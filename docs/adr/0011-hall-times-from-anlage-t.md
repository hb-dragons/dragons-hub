# Hall times are modeled from the Anlage T; the finder still shows, never decides

ADR-0010 left the club's hall times out of the model: "no booking" could not be read as "hall free", so the alternative-date finder showed the booking situation and left the hall to the staff member. The gap showed up somewhere else first. The club rents its halls from the city on an "Anlage T zum Mietantrag" per hall — every Saturday and Sunday of the season with the window the club wants, a dash, or the city's closure note — and nothing compared that sheet with the game plan. A home game on a day or at a time the club had not asked for stayed invisible until someone read both by hand.

Decided 2026-09-16: hall times are a table of their own, `venue_hall_times`, one row per venue and day, imported from the sheet through the admin venue page (parsed in the browser, typed JSON to the API) and editable by hand. A booking is judged against the hall time of its day at read time — covered, partial, uncovered or closed — and the result is shown on the bookings list, the game plan and the finder. A booking that moves out of `covered`, whether because sync re-windowed it or because a new sheet took the time away, emits `booking.hall_time.missing` once. The sheet is replaced whole inside its own date span on every import; a manual row survives only on a day the sheet says nothing about.

The finder's rule from ADR-0010 stands: it ranks days by how little they still need from the city and flags, it does not exclude. Amended 2026-10-25 (hall occupancy, bookings no longer stored): for a home game the finder lists every one of our halls per candidate day, with its hall time and the home games already there, and derives a suggested kickoff from the hall time — the first slot inside it that the moved game fits into next to those games. The tiers are: fits into a held hall time, a hall time held but the game does not fit, no hall time held, every hall closed. A suggestion stays a suggestion; the staff member still picks the day and the hall. School and public holidays arrive as the sheet's closure notes and are not modeled beyond that. Generating the Anlage T back out of the bookings was considered and left out; the club fills the city's form itself.
